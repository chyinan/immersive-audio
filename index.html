My Workflow

DAW: Logic Pro & Adobe Audition
Monitoring: AirPods Pro (2nd generation)
Renderer: Apple Spatial Audio / Head Tracking

This setup allows me to constantly reference what real listeners will actually hear.

I care deeply about how sound feels, not just how it measures.

Focus Areas

High Dynamic Range Instrument Separation Emotional Movement Spatial Balance Realistic Sound Field Natural Voices

What I'm Aiming For


My goal is simple:
To make the listening experience feel like it has stepped into another dimension.

Not louder. Not wider just for the sake of it.
But more immersive, more alive, and more emotionally detailed than stereo.

I want listeners to notice small things — a breath, a movement, a change in distance —
and feel that something is different, even if they can't explain why.

Commissions & Collaboration

I currently accept small-scale Atmos mixing requests in the following situations:

1

Music Enthusiasts

If you love a piece of music that only exists in stereo, you can provide the stereo track and I can attempt an AI-assisted stem separation, then re-mix it into an immersive Atmos version.

2

Independent Musicians

If you have access to the original multitrack project, I can help you explore how your music might translate into Dolby Atmos.
